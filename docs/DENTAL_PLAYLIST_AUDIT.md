# YouTube dental CAD/CAM playlist audit

Audit date: 2026-09-16. The list below records every playlist position visible in the public playlist data, including repeated video entries. It records metadata and checks caption-track availability; it is not a claim that every video was watched or that unavailable narration was reviewed.

## What the playlists cover

* exocad DentalCAD TRAINING Playlist (exocad GmbH): 34 entries, 34 unique video IDs. The sequence spans DentalDB basics, copings/cutback, crown interiors, tooth copying, bridges, Smile Creator, abutments, attachments, denture and framework workflows, bars, and exocam.
* Exocad Training (WIXUR Dental Academy): 64 entries, 61 unique video IDs. The visible titles span monolithic and cutback crowns, onlays/overlays/veneers, waxups and tooth libraries, bridges/connectors, custom abutments and implant crowns, gingiva, virtual articulator/dynamic occlusion, and model/denture workflows. Three placements are repeated video IDs.

These are product-workflow tutorials, not a comprehensive dental anatomy curriculum. I enumerated all 95 unique video IDs, but could not retrieve full transcripts. An independent reviewer reported 11 caption tracks on the first exocad video and 1 on the first WIXUR video; the public timedtext listing returned empty for every ID, so it is not treated as proof that captions are absent. Later watch-page requests returned YouTube automated-traffic CAPTCHA pages, and I stopped rather than bypassing them. Transcript contents and visual details are therefore unverified for every item.

## Evidence limit

Playlist positions and public titles were inventoried, but video narration, on-screen dimensions, clinical decisions, and exact software settings were not independently verified because transcript retrieval was unavailable and watch-page access later returned a CAPTCHA. Treat video content as unreviewed unless separately watched by a qualified reviewer.

* [NCBI: developmental disturbances of teeth, anomalies of structure](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK574516/)
* [Expert consensus on diagnosis and management of tooth developmental anomalies](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12816143/)
* The installed tooth morphology skills include an FDI-indexed atlas of permanent anterior teeth, premolars, molars, and primary dentition, plus a separate non-exhaustive anomaly reference. These sources inform morphology recognition only; they do not certify a patient-specific restoration.
