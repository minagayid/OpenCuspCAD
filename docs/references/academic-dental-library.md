# Academic dental and scan-format library

The links below were selected as study references for the local design skills. OpenCuspCAD summarizes concepts and links to the source; it does not copy textbook chapters or redistribute source images.

## Anatomy and morphology

- [NCBI Bookshelf: Anatomy, Head and Neck, Permanent Dentition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK570590/) — open medical reference on permanent tooth morphology and dentition.
- [NCBI Bookshelf: Anatomy, Head and Neck, Primary Dentition](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK573074/) — open medical reference on primary teeth.
- [NCBI Bookshelf: Anatomy, Head and Neck, Teeth](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557543/) — anatomy overview and terminology.
- [OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e: The Mouth, Pharynx, and Esophagus](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-3-the-mouth-pharynx-and-esophagus) — openly licensed anatomy overview; consult the source license before reuse.
- [Oral Facial Anatomy Online: Tooth Morphology](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/oralfacialonline/chapter/tooth-morphology/) — structured morphology study material.
- [Oral Facial Anatomy Online: Tooth Morphology Part A](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/oralfacialonline/chapter/tooth-morphology-part-a/) — permanent dentition morphology.
- [Oral Facial Anatomy Online: Primary Tooth Morphology Part B](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/oralfacialonline/chapter/tooth-morphology-primary-part-b/) — primary dentition morphology.
- [Oral Facial Anatomy Online: Overview of Dentitions](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/oralfacialonline/chapter/overview-of-dentitions/) — dentition and notation context.

## Digital dentistry and scan data

- [Intraoral scanners in dentistry: a review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5727697/) — review discussing digital impressions and common mesh formats including STL, OBJ, and PLY.
- [A comparison of 3D file formats for digital dentistry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42213258/) — recent study comparing PLY, STL, and OBJ in a study-specific workflow; do not generalize its findings beyond its methods.
- [Teeth3DS+: a large-scale 3D dental scan dataset](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.06094) and [dataset page](https://crns-smartvision.github.io/teeth3ds) — research context for annotated dental surfaces; review dataset terms before use.
